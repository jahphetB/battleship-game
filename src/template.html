Naval Command / Mission Briefing

Welcome aboard, Commander.

Find the enemy fleet before they find yours.

Your orders

  1. Command five ships (lengths 5, 4, 3, 3, 2) on a 10 × 10 grid.
  2. Randomize your fleet. Select Start Battle, or Confirm Fleet in two-player mode.
  3. Take turns firing one shot. Hits and misses stay marked; you cannot fire at the same cell twice.
  4. Sink every enemy ship to win. Enemy positions stay hidden during battle.
