Naval Command / Mission Briefing

Welcome aboard, Commander.

Find the enemy fleet before they find yours.

Your orders

  1. Command five ships (lengths 5, 4, 3, 3, 2) on a 10 × 10 grid.
  2. Randomize your fleet, or drag ships into place. Rotate as needed. You can also select a ship and click its starting cell.
  3. Place all five ships, then select Start Battle (or Confirm Fleet for two players).
  4. Take turns firing one shot. Hits and misses stay marked; you cannot fire at the same cell twice.
  5. Sink every enemy ship to win. Enemy positions stay hidden during battle.
